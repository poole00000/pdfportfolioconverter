    What “preserve signatures” means

    The signature appearance is rendered into each page so it stays visible in the single PDF. Combining separately signed PDFs necessarily changes the combined document, so those signatures cannot remain cryptographically valid on the newly merged pages. When “Embed originals” is checked, the exact original signed PDFs are also placed in the output’s Attachments panel and remain independently verifiable.
